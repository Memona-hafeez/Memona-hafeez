## About me

I have been building AI systems for clients for a little over two years. Most of my work is RAG: getting a model to answer from a company's own documents without making things up. In one client system I reduced hallucinations and improved retrieval by changing how documents were chunked, embedded and prompted, and the client's own testing confirmed it.

I also build agents and automations. In n8n I have integrated more than ten third-party platforms, including QuickBooks, Twilio, WhatsApp Business, AWS SES, Pinecone and VAPI, and I deploy my FastAPI services with Docker on Render and AWS.

Outside client work I research security. My paper on QRShield, a system that catches both tampered QR images and malicious links, is prepared for *IEEE Access*.

## Research

### [QRShield](https://github.com/Memona-hafeez/QRShield) &nbsp;`IEEE Access manuscript`

A QR code can be dangerous in two ways: the image itself may have been altered, or it may simply point to a bad site. QRShield checks both. The first part looks for manipulation in the image, the second runs the decoded URL through a classifier trained on a public malicious-URL dataset. The repo has the code, the training notebooks, and a script that regenerates the dataset structure.
